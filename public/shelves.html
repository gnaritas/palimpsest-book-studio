Books

$7 to $25, on the shelves

The studio opened with books priced $7 to $25 — a range meant for grabbing one on a whim and coming back for the next. Creative Loafing Tampa, which covered the opening, lists the range as part of what the shop carries.

book pricing per Creative Loafing Tampa, November 2026
Zines

The small-press shelf

Zines share the shelves with the books. If you came for the printed matter — stapled, small-run, made by hand — Palimpsest keeps that tradition in print, in the room, where you can pick it up.

Consignment

Select consignment pieces

Beyond the books and zines, the studio carries select consignment pieces — items placed on Palimpsest’s shelves to be sold through the shop.

Why paper

The analog argument

This is what the studio means by analog culture: a shop where the inventory gets read with your eyes, turned with your hands, and taken home without a screen getting involved.
