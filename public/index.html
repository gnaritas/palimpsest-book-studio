A book studio for readers and writers.

Books · zines · select consignmentOpen Friday–Sunday, noon–4 p.m.Room 200, Kress Contemporary

Palimpsest is a new book studio on the second floor of Kress Contemporary in Ybor City — books priced $7 to $25, zines and select consignment pieces, plus a programming calendar of readings and special events. The studio’s whole point is analog culture: the written word, held in your hands.

Behind the desk

Micheal Hooker & Mitzi Jo Gordon

The people behind Palimpsest are no strangers to lit-minded Tampeños: Micheal Hooker ran Lector, the Tampa book and wine bar, and Mitzi Jo Gordon ran the Bluebird Books Bus.

The studio is what they launched next — built, in their words to Creative Loafing Tampa, to honor a combined passion for nontraditional spaces. The bright-orange room inside Kress Contemporary is the newest of them.

Before you visit

Questions folks ask

When are you open?

The studio keeps open hours Friday through Sunday, noon to 4 p.m., as reported by Creative Loafing Tampa. If your timing doesn’t fit those hours, email palimpsestreads@gmail.com and ask.

Where exactly is Palimpsest?

Room 200 on the second floor of Kress Contemporary at 1624 E 7th Ave, Ybor City, Tampa, FL 33605 — the same floor as galleries, studios and a black box theatre. The map in the Find it section below the FAQ pins the front door.

What can I buy?

Books priced $7 to $25, zines, and select consignment pieces — the same offerings the studio opened with on November 19, 2026.

Is it a bookstore or something else?

Creative Loafing Tampa called it “more than a store” — a book studio that pairs the shelves with a programming calendar for readers and writers.

How do I reach you?

Email palimpsestreads@gmail.com — the published inbox on Kress Contemporary’s partner page — or use the contact form, and the note lands in the same inbox.
