palimpsestOur story

The name is the mission.

A palimpsest is a writing surface scraped clean and written over — still carrying traces of what came before. That’s what the studio is for: keeping earlier forms of the written word alive in a digital town.

Say the name

Pal·imp·sest — five syllables, two histories

The studio leans on the word’s layers — it even hyphenates it, the way Creative Loafing Tampa did when it covered the opening. A book that gets resold, a zine passed hand to hand, a page reused: everything here keeps the trace of its earlier form.

Behind the desk

Micheal Hooker & Mitzi Jo Gordon

Micheal Hooker ran Lector, the Tampa book and wine bar. Mitzi Jo Gordon ran the Bluebird Books Bus. Palimpsest is what the two launched next — built, as they told Creative Loafing Tampa, to honor their combined passion for nontraditional spaces.

founder histories per Creative Loafing Tampa, November 2026
The room

An orange room in an arts building

Room 200, second floor of Kress Contemporary — a Ybor City building that hosts galleries, a black box theatre, commercial photography studios and a fiber art boutique. The studio’s own walls are bright orange, in the photographs Dave Decker made the week it opened.

Opening night

November 19, 2026

Palimpsest opened with readings on opening night — books priced $7 to $25, zines and select consignment pieces on the shelves from day one. The studio’s open hours since: Friday through Sunday, noon to 4 p.m.
