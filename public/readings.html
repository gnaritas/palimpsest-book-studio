Events for readers and writers

Creative Loafing Tampa describes Palimpsest as promoting analog culture “via what’s on the shelves and on the programming calendar for readers and writers.” The calendar is the studio half of the studio — readings, gatherings and special events hosted in the orange room on Kress’s second floor.
