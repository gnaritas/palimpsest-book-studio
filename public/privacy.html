Privacy

This site sets no cookies and loads no advertising or tracking scripts. Nothing is counted with analytics.

The only information collected is what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, an optional phone number, an optional email address, and your message. It is used for one purpose — answering you — and kept only as long as needed to reply and to remember what you asked.

When you click through to Instagram or Google Maps, those pages run under their own privacy policies.
